# Privacy Policy for Discord Bot

**Effective Date:** September 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how our Discord bot ("Bot") collects, uses, and protects user data. We value your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly.

---

## 1. Information We Collect
To function properly, the Bot may collect and store minimal data required for its operation:

* **User Information:**
  * Discord User IDs (used to store preferences, track usage limits, or manage administrative rights).
  * Username & Discriminator/Avatar (temporarily cached for dashboard display).
* **Server (Guild) Data:**
  * Guild IDs, Channel IDs, Role IDs (used to save server configurations, music settings, or prefixes).
* **Command & Usage Logs:**
  * Operational logs (e.g., error tracking, command execution history) to maintain stability and diagnose bugs.
* **Dashboard Session Data:**
  * Web session cookies or OAuth2 tokens stored securely when logging into the Bot web dashboard.

> **Note:** The Bot does **not** permanently log, store, or monitor private text message content or voice conversations.

## 2. How We Use the Data
The collected data is used strictly for:
* Providing core functionality (e.g., loading server settings, maintaining music queues).
* Authenticating users on the web dashboard via Discord OAuth2.
* Preventing abuse, spam, and unauthorized API usage.
* Diagnosing technical issues and improving performance.

## 3. Data Storage & Protection
* Data is stored securely on private server databases.
* Access to databases and internal logs is restricted exclusively to authorized bot administrators.
* We implement industry-standard encryption practices for dashboard web connections (HTTPS/TLS).

## 4. Data Sharing & Third Parties
We **do not** sell, trade, or share your personal data with third-party marketers or advertisers. Limited data may interact with trusted third-party APIs (e.g., Discord API, YouTube/Spotify metadata APIs) solely for feature execution.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* **Retention:** Data is kept as long as the Bot remains in your server or as needed to maintain your saved configurations.
* **Deletion:**
  * Removing (kicking/banning) the Bot from a server will render guild settings inactive.
  * Server owners or individual users may request full deletion of stored data by contacting the developer.

## 6. Policy Updates
We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Continued use of the Bot following updates implies acceptance of the new privacy practices.

---

## Contact & Data Requests
For privacy inquiries or to request data removal, please contact the bot administrator or reach out via our official support channel.
